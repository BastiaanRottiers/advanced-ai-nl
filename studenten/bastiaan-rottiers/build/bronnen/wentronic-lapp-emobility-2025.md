MOBILITY DOCK
SMALL AND HANDY - IDEAL FOR ON THE ROAD
The LAPP MOBILITY DOCK is a practical solution for supplying electric vehicles with  power even without a charging pole, a public or a home charging station. It is the  perfect solution as an emergency charger. It allows for simple, mobile charging via a  standard Type E/F mains socket and is ideal for on the road or in the garage at home.

CHARGE ANYTIME AND ANYWHERE
